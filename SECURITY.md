## Reporting a Vulnerability

We take the security of Cloudflare Email Sender seriously. If you believe you have discovered a vulnerability, please report it to us responsibly.

### How to Report
* **Preferred:** Submit a report privately via GitHub's [Private Vulnerability Reporting](https://docs.github.com/code-security/security-advisories/guidance-on-reporting-and-fixing-vulnerabilities/privately-reporting-a-vulnerability) under the **Security** tab of this repository.
* **Alternative:** Submit your findings through our contact form at [potomactech.net/contact](https://potomactech.net/contact/).

Please include as much detail as possible to help us reproduce the issue:
* Steps to reproduce or proof-of-concept (PoC)
* Impact of the vulnerability
* Any proposed mitigations or fixes

### What to Expect
* **Response Time:** We aim to acknowledge receipt of your report within **48 hours**.
* **Updates:** We will keep you updated on our progress as we triage and validate the vulnerability.
* **Disclosure:** We kindly ask that you keep the issue confidential and allow us reasonable time to release a patch before disclosing it publicly.
